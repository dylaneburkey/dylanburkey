Toothless - Autonomous Robot

Description

Designed and fabricated an autonomous mobile robot integrating machined, 3D-printed, laser-cut, pneumatic, actuating and sensor components with Arduino-based mechatronic control. Placed 2nd out of 70+ teams in GT's Creative Decisions & Design competition.

Applied DFM/DFA and DFMEA through iterative prototyping of components with GD&T specifications in SOLIDWORKS/OnShape.
